NP Membership Benefits

Designed for nonprofit organizations with no membership fee.

  • Access to community networking events.
  • Basic business directory listing.
  • Access to selected educational resources.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Essential support for small and growing businesses.

  • All NP membership benefits.
  • Discounts on selected chamber events.
  • Access to business training sessions.

Silver Membership Benefits

More opportunities to promote your business.

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Priority access to networking events.
  • Additional advertising opportunities.
  • Eligibility for business spotlights.

Gold Membership Benefits

Premium benefits for established businesses.

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Premium homepage advertising opportunities.
  • Exclusive business networking events.
  • Priority promotional opportunities.
